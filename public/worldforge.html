Frontier rules

  1. Creative: build with unlimited materials and no costs.
  2. Adventure: gather blocks into your backpack, craft materials, trade and spend resources when building.
  3. Connected plots: simple stone lanes link the inner landmarks and the eight-direction outer settlement ring. Approach a glowing claim beacon and press E to occupy that plot.
  4. AI Builders: occupying a plot spawns its named AI Builder. Watch the builder construct a lodge in stages; the claimed area also becomes editable.
  5. Plot access: near your occupied plot, press P to cycle Private, Friends and Public access. This is a local access prototype; real friend identity and permission enforcement arrives with network accounts.
  6. Outer settlements: spaced workshops, homes, halls, gardens, keeps and an observatory surround the frontier, with Aurora Guild Citadel to the north.
  7. Frontier Gate: approach the glowing gate near camp and press E. Gather up to 24 resources and return through the expedition gate within two minutes.
  8. If a gate collapses, common materials return safely but unprotected rare resources are lost.
  9. The people walking around are named AI residents with settlement roles—not online players.
  10. The dashed boundary is your current buildable territory. Earn coins and complete objectives to expand it.
  11. Your settlement progresses from Camp to Homestead, Village, Town, City and Civilisation.
  12. There is no forced combat. Exploration, construction and settlement growth are the objectives.
  13. Online accounts, co-building and ranked matches require the later network release and are never faked locally.
